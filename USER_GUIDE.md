# 1. Introduction

Welcome to XSOLA.

XSOLA is a cloud-based Smart Solar Energy Management Platform that allows you to:

- Monitor your solar system
- View battery status
- Track energy production
- Pay subscriptions online
- Receive alerts
- Download receipts
- Control supported devices remotely (where permitted)

Whether you are using XSOLA at home, in a business, or through an institution, the platform provides a single place to manage your solar services.

---

# 2. Getting Started

To use XSOLA, you need:

- An internet-connected device (phone, tablet, or computer)
- A supported web browser
- An active XSOLA account
- An installed XSOLA-enabled solar system (for monitoring features)

---

# 3. Creating an Account

If self-registration is enabled:

1. Open the XSOLA website.
2. Click **Sign Up**.
3. Enter:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone Number
   - Password
4. Accept the Terms of Service.
5. Click **Create Account**.

You may receive an email or SMS verification, depending on system configuration.

---

# 4. Logging In

1. Open the XSOLA login page.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Login**.

Successful login takes you to your dashboard.

If you forget your password, use the **Forgot Password** option (when available) or contact support.

# 20. Troubleshooting

## Cannot Log In

Possible causes:

- Incorrect password
- Account not activated
- Internet connection unavailable

Solution:

- Verify your credentials.
- Reset your password if available.
- Contact support.

---

## Payment Not Reflected

Possible causes:

- Payment verification still in progress
- Network interruption

Solution:

- Wait a few minutes.
- Refresh your dashboard.
- Contact support if the issue persists.

---

## Device Offline

Possible causes:

- Power interruption
- Wi-Fi unavailable
- Hardware fault

Solution:

- Check your power source.
- Verify your internet connection.
- Contact support if the device remains offline.

---

## No Telemetry Data

Possible causes:

- Device disconnected
- Sensor malfunction
- Temporary communication issue

Solution:

- Wait for the next update cycle.
- Contact support if no data appears after several intervals.
