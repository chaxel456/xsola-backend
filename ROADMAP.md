# XSOLA Product Roadmap

## Introduction

The XSOLA roadmap defines the strategic direction of the platform over multiple releases. It outlines planned features, technical improvements, infrastructure upgrades, IoT enhancements, and business expansion goals.

This roadmap serves as a guide for developers, project managers, investors, and stakeholders, helping everyone understand the long-term vision of the XSOLA ecosystem.

---

# Vision

To become Africa's leading cloud-based Smart Solar Management Platform, enabling intelligent, secure, and scalable solar energy management through IoT, cloud computing, automation, and artificial intelligence.

---

# Mission

To simplify the deployment, monitoring, billing, and maintenance of solar energy systems through an integrated software and hardware platform.
